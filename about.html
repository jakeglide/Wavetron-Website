OUR COMPANY

Built for Mission-Critical Environments

Wavetron LLC is a controls consulting firm specializing in BMS/EPMS for data centers and other critical facilities. We bridge the gap between design, construction, and operations with independent, hands-on expertise focused on dependable system performance.

From commissioning and integration through turnover, we help project teams identify issues early, make informed decisions, and deliver systems that are ready for the demands of day-to-day operations.

OUR FOCUSControls, integration, commissioning, and operational performance for critical environments.

OUR APPROACHIndependent, detail-driven, and solutions-oriented—with practical guidance at every stage.

OUR GOALReliable systems, fewer issues, and long-term efficiency for our clients.
